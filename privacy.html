Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Shashi Raghuvanshi ("we", "us", or "our") operates ragmispathmaker.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or engage our consulting services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and company name when you reach out to us via email or a contact form.
  • Project details — technical information you share about your software, infrastructure, or processes during a consulting engagement.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics such as pages visited, time on site, browser type, and referring URL. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide consulting services.
  • Send project-related communications (proposals, reports, invoices).
  • Improve our website and services through aggregate analytics.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined above, or as required by law. Project-related information is retained for the duration of the engagement plus 12 months, after which it is securely deleted unless you request otherwise.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. All communications containing sensitive project data are handled over encrypted channels. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

This website uses only essential, first-party cookies required for basic site functionality. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services (such as GitHub Pages for hosting or email providers for communication). These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.
