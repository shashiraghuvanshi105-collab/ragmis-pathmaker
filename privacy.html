1. Introduction

Shashi Raghuvanshi ("we", "us", or "our") operates ragmispathmaker.com and sells digital products for software testing and DevOps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or buy our products.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — your name and email address when you contact us or make a purchase.
  • Order information — the products you bought, the date and amount of your order, and your billing country. Payments are handled by our payment provider, Polar, and we do not see or store your card details.
  • Usage data — anonymised analytics such as pages visited, time on site, browser type, and referring URL. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver the products you bought by email and send you product updates you are entitled to.
  • Respond to enquiries, support requests, and refund requests.
  • Improve our website and products through aggregate analytics.
  • Comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed to deliver your purchase, provide updates and support, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. After that, it is securely deleted unless you ask us to delete it sooner and the law allows it.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet, including email, is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

This website uses only essential, first-party cookies required for basic site functionality. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies on our own pages. Our payment provider may use its own cookies on its checkout pages, as described in its privacy policy.

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Polar Software, Inc. — our Merchant of Record, which processes payments, handles taxes, and keeps your order and billing details.
  • Email provider — used to send your purchased files and to answer your messages.
  • Hosting provider — such as GitHub Pages, which serves this website.

We encourage you to review their policies.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your data.
  • Request a portable copy of your data.

Our products are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
