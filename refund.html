Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

48-Hour Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the delivered work, you can request a full refund within 48 hours of delivery—no questions asked.

1. Eligibility

Refund requests are accepted under the following conditions:

  • The request is made within 48 hours of the final deliverable being shared with you.
  • The engagement was for one of our standard plans (Starter at $100, Professional at $250, or Enterprise at $500).
  • Custom-scoped engagements follow the refund terms defined in their individual statement of work.

2. Refund Process

1

Request

Email us at with your engagement details and reason.

2

Review

We review your request promptly and confirm eligibility.

3

Refund

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.

3. Full Refund

A full refund of the engagement fee is issued when:

  • The request is within the 48-hour window after final delivery.
  • Work has not yet begun (cancellation before kickoff).

4. Partial Refund

A partial refund may be offered when:

  • The engagement is terminated mid-way through the project. You will be charged only for work completed, and the remainder is refunded.
  • Deliverables have been partially used or implemented.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • The refund request is made more than 48 hours after delivery.
  • All deliverables have been fully accepted and signed off.
  • Delays were caused by the client's failure to provide required access, information, or feedback.
  • The service has been fully rendered and completed as agreed.

6. Disputes

If you disagree with a refund decision, we encourage you to reach out for a discussion. We are committed to finding a fair resolution. If an agreement cannot be reached, the dispute will be handled under the terms outlined in our Terms of Service.
